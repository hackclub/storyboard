# Prizes

Every prize is earned with tracked hours. Hit a tier, pick something from it. Higher tiers open up as you log more time.

## Prize tiers

| Tier     | Hours | Example rewards                          |
| -------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Stickers | free  | Limited-edition Storyboardtober stickers |
| Tier 1   | ~10   | Fat cat plushie, Steam dev license       |
| Tier 2   | ~25   | Mechanical keyboard, sticker sheets      |
| Tier 3   | ~40   | Screenless drawing tablet                |
| Tier 4   | ~60   | Drawing tablet with a screen             |

Exact thresholds and stock can shift during the event. The home page always reflects the current list.

## What counts

- **Coding** your game, tracked by Hackatime.
- **Art and writing**, tracked by Lapse.

Hours you can't demonstrate — work done before a tracker was running, for example — generally can't be counted. When unsure, ask in the `#storyboard` channel.

## Claiming a prize

1. Reach the hours for a tier.
2. Submit your project (see [Submitting Your Project](/docs/submitting)).
3. Pick your reward when prompted.

Prizes ship after the event wraps, so there's no rush to claim the moment you qualify.
